I am a Research Scientist at Canva in San Francisco. I am completing my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Notre Dame, advised by Prof. Yiyu Shi. I also work closely with Dr. Jianxu Chen (ISAS). I received my B.Eng. from Zhejiang University in 2022.

My research centers on post-training, representation learning, generative AI, and agentic AI, with applications in medical and microscopy image analysis.
