I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Notre Dame, advised by Prof. Yiyu Shi. I also work closely with Dr. Jianxu Chen (ISAS). I received my B.S. from Zhejiang University in 2022.

My research centers on post-training, representation learning, generative AI, and agentic AI, with applications in medical and microscopy image analysis.
